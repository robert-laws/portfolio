About Me

Librarian, technologist, and lifelong learner

I'm Robert Laws, a librarian who has always been fascinated by the intersection of information science and technology. Throughout my career, I've sought ways to leverage emerging tools and platforms to enhance how people discover, access, and interact with information.

My journey has led me to embrace artificial intelligence, multimedia production, and web technologies as natural extensions of traditional library work. I believe that the core mission of librarianship—connecting people with the knowledge they need—remains constant, even as the methods evolve.

My Approach

I approach each project with curiosity and a willingness to experiment. Whether I'm developing an AI-powered tool, creating a multimedia presentation, or exploring new ways to organize information, I focus on practical applications that make a real difference.

I'm particularly interested in how AI can augment human capabilities rather than replace them, and how multimedia can make complex information more accessible and engaging.

Background

With a foundation in library and information science, I've expanded my skills to include web development, video and audio production, and AI tools. This combination allows me to create innovative solutions that bridge the gap between traditional information management and modern technology.

I enjoy sharing what I learn through presentations, discussions, and hands-on projects. Feel free to explore my work and reach out if you'd like to connect.
